## Who we are

MBSC Firm provides RJSC, income tax, VAT and audit support services from its office in Chattogram, Bangladesh. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and what we do with it.

## What we collect

When you send an enquiry through the form on this website we receive:

- your name
- the phone number or email address you give us
- the service you are asking about, if you choose one
- the message you write

We also record the date and time of the enquiry and the internet address (IP address) it was sent from. This helps us stop spam and misuse of the form.

If you call us, email us or message us on WhatsApp, we receive whatever you choose to share in that conversation. WhatsApp, your phone company and your email provider handle those messages under their own policies.

## How we use it

We use your information to:

- reply to your enquiry
- prepare and provide the service you ask for
- keep a record of our dealings with you, as a professional firm is expected to

We do not sell your information. We do not send marketing messages unless you have asked for them.

## Who can see it

Your enquiry is read by the people at MBSC Firm who deal with enquiries and client work. It is stored with the company that hosts our website and sent through our email provider so that it reaches us.

When we act for you, we share the information that a filing requires with the relevant authority, such as RJSC or the National Board of Revenue, on your instruction.

We may also disclose information when the law requires us to.

## Cookies

This website uses a small number of cookies that are needed for it to work, for example to keep the enquiry form secure. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

The map on the contact page is provided by Google. It is only loaded if you press "Show map"; if you do, Google may set its own cookies.

## How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to deal with your request and to keep proper business records. When we no longer need an enquiry we delete it.

## Your choices

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. We will do so unless we are required to keep it. Use the details on our Contact page to reach us.

## Security

Enquiries are sent to us over an encrypted connection and can only be read by authorised staff who sign in to our system. No method of storing or sending information is completely secure, but we take reasonable care to protect yours.

## Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information we will update this page. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

## Contact

If you have a question about this policy or about your information, contact us using the details on our Contact page.
