### Notes on the Unsplash pictures

- The [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) allows free commercial use without attribution.
- Photographer names are not recorded here. Unsplash's search pages could not be reached from the build environment, so the photo pages that name the photographers were not looked up. The IDs above are from the standard Unsplash library, not the paid Unsplash+ collection, which uses a different ID format.
